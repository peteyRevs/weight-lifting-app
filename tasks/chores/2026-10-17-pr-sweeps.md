# The text-size and setting sweeps on the hosted runner (2026-10-17)

Branch `worktree-agent-a15b38a04e54911f6`, cut from `main` at `77b33ec`. It follows the seeds chore
(`tasks/chores/2026-10-09-pr-lane-time.md`): one `TEST_RUNNER_` variable in `pr.yml`, read by the tests, so
that the hosted job `Check` runs less and every other lane runs as before. Pete's ruling: move most of the
text-size and hosted-view sweeps off the hosted PR run, which costs least. They still run whole in the full
local gate (`scripts/check.sh`, green before every merge) and in the nightly. The ruling first kept
`LEDGER_XCODEBUILD_DEADLINE` at 2,520 s and every timeout as it was. Once the cut fell short of a 5 min margin
(below), the orchestrator ruled to keep the cut and also raise the deadline to 3,120 s ("The deadline").

## The problem

On `main` the iOS test step took about 1,255–1,293 s on a developer Mac at the PR plan's 200 seeds (F10 S7's
gate of 2026-10-14: 1,267 s, 42,168 cases). The runner took 2.0–3.2 times the Mac's time on comparable commits on 2026-10-09 (seeds chore), and a PR's first run builds cold
(about 1,150 s) after about 125 s of script tests, so every first run reached the deadline, whatever its
code. With the runner's 40 seeds the same step took 903 s on the Mac (below): about 1,800 s on the runner, still
past the 1,235 s a cold run has left for tests.

## The change

`TEST_RUNNER_LEDGER_SWEEP: pr` in `Check`'s step, beside the seeds; `xcodebuild` hands it to the test runner as
`LEDGER_SWEEP`. One helper reads it: `TestSweep` in `LedgerTestSupport/Sweeps/TestSweep.swift`.

- `TestSweep.isReduced`: `LEDGER_SWEEP == "pr"`. Unset, as in the local gate, the nightly and `swift test`,
  every sweep is whole: each helper returns its input unchanged.
- `TestSweep.subset(_:keeping:)`: the whole list, or in a reduced run the elements kept, in order (the first if
  none is, so that no test is left with no case to run). A reduced list is always a subset of the whole one,
  so the runner can fail nothing the full gate passes, unless a test reads a swept list's count or the
  positions of its elements.
- `TestSweep.sizes(_:also:)`: of a list of text sizes, the representatives `.xSmall`, `.large`, `.xxxLarge` and
  `.accessibility5`, and any `also` (the sizes a finding was seen at). Every current list starts and ends on
  a representative.

## The deadline

A first run: about 125 s of script tests, a cold build of 1,140–1,221 s, 10 s of catalogs and boot, then the
tests. With the sweeps cut alone (576 s) and the deadline at 2,520 s: at a 2× runner, the low end of the 2.0–3.2×
seen, the tests take about 1,150 s, so a cold run ends at 2,437–2,508 s, inside the deadline by 12–83 s, and at
the high end it is stopped. Before the cut the same run needed about 1,800 s of tests at 2× and could not
finish.

**Raised to 3,120 s (orchestrator's ruling, second commit).** A run stopped by the deadline costs all of its
minutes plus a re-run. A green run bills only what it uses, so headroom costs nothing until a run hangs (and
then 10 minutes more than at 2,520 s). The factor is 2.5×. On 2026-10-09 the runner took 2.0 to 3.2 times
the Mac's time on comparable commits (seeds chore, "Measured"), and 3.6 times for #49's second attempt
(1,497 s against 419 s, a Mac run without #49's S9 suites). 2.5× sits past the comparable range and short of
that outlier. Cold build 1,221 s + 125 s of script tests + 576 s × 2.5 = 1,440 s of tests is about 2,786 s;
plus 5 min, about 3,086 s, set to 3,120 s. After fix round 1 (595 s) the same sum is 1,221 + 125 + 1,488 =
2,834 s, 286 s inside: the margin is about 4.8 min, not 5, until the deadline is moved again or the time
falls back. The step's timeout is 54 min (3,240 s, 120 s after the deadline,
the gap check.sh's interrupt relies on to finalize the bundle; WF49 and WF65 check it). The job's is 59 min,
the step's plus the same 5 min for checkout, Xcode, mise, the caches and the failure upload. The other
options (the Day editor's cut measure, the UI smoke off the runner, 30 seeds, a `main` warm-up job) were not
taken.

  runner, the step takes what it took before and the case count tells (a failed run uploads its bundle):
  `FeatureProgramTests` has 3,050 cases with every sweep and 2,559 under `pr`.
- To run what `Check` runs locally: `TEST_RUNNER_LEDGER_SWEEP=pr TEST_RUNNER_LEDGER_PROPERTY_SEED_COUNT=40
  scripts/check.sh --ios-only`. A test that passes whole and fails under `pr` can only be one that reads
  positions or counts of a swept list (as `Layout.sizes.prefix(7)` does).
