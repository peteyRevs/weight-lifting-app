ASSUMED

Barbell strength plus hypertrophy accessories, four days a week, pounds. Programs get built at home; the gym is for logging only.

THE SYSTEM

Paper ground, ink type, one accent. Numbers are the loudest thing on every screen: expanded Archivo, tabular figures, nothing below 15px.

NEW THIS PASS

Exercises can be chained into supersets from the expanded panel, and any exercise can carry a drop set. The live workout shows a superset as one block, logged round by round, and only rests once the last exercise in the round is done.

Phone — setup, programming and review. Change the week or the schedule on Program, then open Today: the session that starts is the one you just wrote. Tap a set row for steppers; tick a set to start the shared rest timer.
APPLE WATCH · 46MM
Watch — six states: before the workout (start from the wrist), set ready, rest, the session list (tap the exercise name), end-workout confirm, and the summary. Weight and reps arrive preset from the phone. Drag the crown to adjust, tap the face to log. Drawn at 1.5× a 46mm display (208 × 248 pt) — divide by 1.5 for real point sizes. Smallest label is 17px = 11pt; the ± buttons are 44px = 29pt, tuned for the crown to do the fine work.
