# R1 — Apple platform research for Ledger (iPhone + Apple Watch lifting log)

Research date: 2026-09-16. Local toolchain at time of research: Xcode 26.6 / Swift 6.3.3 / macOS 26.6.2.

Every claim carries a URL. Items I could not verify against a primary source are marked **UNCONFIRMED**. Where Apple documentation and a third-party summary disagree, Apple wins and the disagreement is called out.

## 0. Decisions that fall out of this research (read this first)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| D1 | **Ship against the iOS 27 / watchOS 27 SDKs with Xcode 27 (Swift 6.4), but set minimum deployment to iOS 26 / watchOS 26.** | Xcode 27 GA'd 2026-09-14 and the App Store will require the 27 SDKs from April 2027 anyway. watchOS 27 drops Series 6/7/8/SE 2/Ultra 1; watchOS 26 keeps them. iOS 26 is the version that brought `HKWorkoutSession`/`HKLiveWorkoutBuilder` to iPhone, RelevanceKit/Controls to watchOS, and Liquid Glass — everything Ledger needs exists at 26. (§1) |
| D2 | **The watch is the primary `HKWorkoutSession`; the phone is a mirror.** Phone-initiated *primary* sessions with a watch mirror do not exist. Phone can *request* a watch start via `HKHealthStore.startWatchApp(toHandle:)`. | `startMirroringToCompanionDevice` is watchOS-only (watchOS 10+); Apple engineer confirmed "the watch continues to remain the main device"; WWDC25 says start on watch, mirror to phone. (§2) |
| D3 | **Use HealthKit mirroring for the live workout channel; use WatchConnectivity for everything non-live (programs, history reconciliation).** Design for the mirror dropping: watch persists the log locally and is the source of truth; phone shows "reconnecting"; reconcile after the session over WatchConnectivity. | Mirroring auto-syncs pause/resume/end and launches the phone app in the background, but has documented multi-minute delivery gaps when the phone app is suspended, an unresolved iOS/watchOS 26.0.x regression (FB20723311), and a reported ~5% "send never returns" hang. (§2) |
| D4 | **Call `session.prepare()` and wait for `.prepared` before `startMirroringToCompanionDevice()` and before any `sendToRemoteWorkoutSession`.** | Apple DTS workaround for the 26.0.x mirroring bug; also avoids "Remote session delegate is not set up". (§2.5) |
| D5 | **Start the workout Live Activity inside `workoutSessionMirroringStartHandler` on the phone; drive the rest timer with `Text(timerInterval:)` and update (not restart) the activity from the background.** | `Activity.request` normally needs foreground, but HealthKit's mirroring launch explicitly gives the phone app "10 seconds to start a live activity". Updates/ends are allowed from the background. A watchOS app cannot start a Live Activity. (§4) |
| D6 | **Rest-timer haptics on the watch: use `WKInterfaceDevice.current().play(_:)` sparingly, and expect heart-rate sampling to pause while the haptic engine runs. Add the `audio` background mode in addition to `workout-processing`.** | Apple docs: haptics stop HR collection until the engine finishes; ≥100 ms between plays; haptics in background require an active workout session; "If your app plays audio or provides haptic feedback during the workout session, you must also add the Audio background mode." (§3.1, §5.3) |
| D7 | **Use `HKWorkoutActivityType.traditionalStrengthTraining` for the session.** Expect generic calorimetry (no activity-specific model) and HR from the watch. Save via the live builder (`endCollection` → `finishWorkout`) so rings are credited; attach set/rep metadata via `addMetadata`. | Only `functionalStrengthTraining` has a documented optimized calorie model; "all other activities" use a sensor-based floor "never lower than the brisk walk burn rate". (§3) |
| D8 | **Do not sync any HealthKit-derived data (HR, calories) through iCloud/CloudKit.** Sync sets/reps/weights only, and treat the HealthKit workout as the phone-readable artifact. | App Review 5.1.3(ii): apps "may not store personal health information in iCloud". (§3.4) |
| D9 | **Use `WKApplication`/`WKApplicationDelegate` (not `WKExtension`).** Implement `handle(_ workoutConfiguration:)` and `handleActiveWorkoutRecovery()`. | `WKExtension`/`WKExtensionDelegate` are deprecated in the watchOS 27 SDK for min target ≥ 9.2. (§5.4) |
| D10 | **Digital Crown weight stepper: `digitalCrownRotation(detent:from:through:by:…onChange:onIdle:)` (watchOS 9+) on a `.focusable()` view with `by:` = plate increment.** | Detent-based API with built-in haptic detents and an `onIdle` callback to commit. (§5.2) |

### 2.2 Which device initiates
- **Watch initiates the mirror; there is no phone-side "start mirroring" call.** `startMirroringToCompanionDevice` exists only on watchOS (table above).
- The phone can *ask* the watch to start: `startWatchApp(toHandle:)` → watch `handle(_:)` → watch builds session → `startMirroringToCompanionDevice()`. Apple's own doc example does exactly this (URL in table).
- Apple Frameworks Engineer, Nov 2023: "Live workout sessions are only available on watchOS and not on iOS. As you've noted, iOS can mirror watch workout sessions now. But the watch continues to remain the main device for workouts." https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/740630
- **iOS 26 / 27:** iPhone can now run its *own* primary `HKWorkoutSession` + `HKLiveWorkoutBuilder` (no HR unless a Bluetooth HR monitor is paired), with Lock-Screen Live Activities and Siri intents. WWDC25 guidance: "If you have a Watch app, be sure to start the workout there to get all available metrics. Just call Start Watch App from the Health Store … be sure to mirror the workout to the iPhone." https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/322/  Nothing in the iOS 27 release notes or WWDC26 changes this. **Conclusion: phone-primary + watch-mirror is still not a supported configuration.**
- WWDC23 session 10023 (still the canonical design): the phone app, launched in the background, "gives my app 10 seconds to start a live activity and call a handler to start mirroring"; "HealthKit takes care of keeping both the primary and the mirrored session states in sync"; pausing/resuming from either side propagates; "Keep in mind that it is important to keep a reference to the retrieved mirrored session on the iPhone app." https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10023/  Sample: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/building-a-multidevice-workout-app

### 2.3 Background / suspension / recovery behaviour (documented)
- iOS side: "your app can go into the background and become suspended. When suspended, HealthKit gathers the data coming from the remote session. When the app resumes, HealthKit sends an array containing all the data objects … During the workout session, HealthKit wakes the iOS app periodically to deliver any cached data objects, **but there might be several minutes between delegate calls.**" (didReceiveDataFromRemoteWorkoutSession doc, URL in table.)
- watchOS side: "the workout session keeps the app running even if it's in the background; however, the system can temporarily suspend the app — for example, if the app uses an excessive amount of CPU in the background." (same doc)
- State callbacks may arrive late: "If your application is suspended, the delegate receives this call after the application resumes … Check the `date` parameter." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutsessiondelegate/workoutsession(_:didchangeto:from:date:)
- Reconnection: "Your app may receive multiple calls to `workoutSessionMirroringStartHandler`. If iPhone and Apple Watch lose their connection in the middle of a workout session, Apple Watch automatically tries to reconnect. Each call has its own `HKWorkoutSession` instance." (handler doc). After `didDisconnectFromRemoteDeviceWithError`, "If the primary workout session is still running, it automatically tries to reconnect."
- Crash recovery: watch — `handleActiveWorkoutRecovery()` → `recoverActiveWorkoutSession()` → re-attach builder/dataSource/delegates. iOS 26+ — `if options.shouldHandleActiveWorkoutRecovery { store.recoverActiveWorkoutSession … }` in the scene-configuration delegate (WWDC25 322).
- Only one watch workout session at a time: a second session (e.g. Apple's Workout app) ends yours with `errorAnotherWorkoutSessionStarted`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutsession

### 2.4 Payload size / frequency limits
- **No documented size or rate limit for `sendToRemoteWorkoutSession`** in the Apple docs fetched (only that the argument is `Data`; Apple's sample uses `NSKeyedArchiver` with secure coding). **UNCONFIRMED: any hard cap.** Practical limits are delivery latency (minutes when the phone app is suspended) and the reliability issues below — so send small, idempotent, versioned event records and re-send state snapshots, not deltas only.

### 2.5 Known gotchas (developer forums / blogs)
1. **iOS 26.0.x / watchOS 26.0.2 mirroring regression** (Oct–Nov 2025, still open in 26.1 RC): phone-started (`startWatchApp`) mirrored sessions connect then drop; watch logs `com.apple.healthkit Code=100 "Failed to send data to remote session."`, phone logs `Code=554 "Primary session is unreachable."`; reproduces with Apple's own sample; FB20723311 marked duplicate, "HealthKit team actively working on it". **Apple DTS workaround:** "making sure `startMirroringToCompanionDevice` is called after the workout session gets prepared or running may be able to work around the issue. You can do so by calling `prepare()`, or waiting until `workoutSession(_:didChangeTo:from:date:)`…" https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/804276  **UNCONFIRMED whether 26.2–27.0 fixed it** (no release note mentions it; the watchOS 27 "Workout" fix concerns Mirrored *Playback*, not sessions).
2. **`sendToRemoteWorkoutSession` sometimes never returns** (no success, no error) for ~5% of workouts and stays broken for that workout; only a reboot of both devices clears it; watchOS 10/11, iOS 17/18; no Apple answer. https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/769355
3. **"Remote session delegate is not set up"** when sending before the session is prepared — call `prepare()` first, then `startMirroringToCompanionDevice()`, then send; retry the first send. https://nonstrict.eu/blog/2024/hkworkoutsession-remote-delegate-not-setup-error/
4. **`workoutSessionMirroringStartHandler` never called** (Apr 2025, iPhone 16 Pro / Ultra 2, unanswered) — assign the handler at the very top of app launch, before anything async. https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/782370
5. `startMirroringToCompanionDevice` can hang without throwing on reconnect attempts (forum 804276, andrewfromCanada). Wrap it in a timeout.
6. Must test on physical devices; the multidevice sample requires `WKCompanionAppBundleIdentifier` to match. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/building-a-multidevice-workout-app

### 2.6 WatchConnectivity comparison
Docs: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchconnectivity/wcsession
| Method | Reachability | Delivery | Wakes counterpart? | Documented limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sendMessage(_:replyHandler:errorHandler:)` / `sendMessageData` | Requires `isReachable`; "Messages are queued serially and delivered in the order in which you sent them." | Immediate, not persisted | **watch→iPhone: yes** ("wakes up the corresponding iOS app in the background and makes it reachable"); **iPhone→watch: no** | `WCError.Code.payloadTooLarge` exists but Apple gives no number. Community-measured **65,536 bytes**. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchconnectivity/wcsession/sendmessage(_:replyhandler:errorhandler:) ; https://blog.martinp7r.com/posts/size-limits-for-watchconnectivity-data-transfers/ |
| `transferUserInfo(_:)` | Not required | Queued, in order, "continues even if the app is suspended"; **not supported in Simulator** | No | Community-measured 65,536 bytes. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchconnectivity/wcsession/transferuserinfo(_:) |
| `updateApplicationContext(_:)` | Not required ("You may call this method when the counterpart is not currently reachable") | Latest-value-wins, delivered opportunistically | No | Community-measured 262,144 bytes. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchconnectivity/wcsession/updateapplicationcontext(_:) |
| `transferFile(_:metadata:)` | Not required | Queued, background | No | Use for bulk history / program JSON. |
- `isReachable` on the watch is true when "the WatchKit extension is running in the foreground, **or is running with a high priority in the background (for example, during a workout session** …)". So during a Ledger workout the phone *can* `sendMessage` to the watch. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchconnectivity/wcsession/isreachable
- Community write-up of the three modes: https://alexanderweiss.dev/blog/2023-01-18-three-ways-to-communicate-via-watchconnectivity
- **Verdict.** (a) Live workout events: HealthKit mirroring is the only channel that also synchronizes session state, launches the phone app in the background and lets the phone show HR — use it, but treat it as lossy (2.3, 2.5) and make the watch authoritative. Optionally mirror the same events over `sendMessage` while `isReachable` for lower latency on the phone UI. (b) Non-live sync (push program to watch, pull completed logs back, settings): `transferUserInfo` for ordered, guaranteed small records; `transferFile` for whole programs/history; `updateApplicationContext` for "current program / next workout" state. Never rely on `sendMessage` for anything the phone must have.

## 3. `HKLiveWorkoutBuilder` on watchOS for strength training; saving; privacy

### 3.1 Live builder facts
- `HKLiveWorkoutBuilder`: watchOS 5+, **iOS 26+ / iPadOS 26+**. `HKLiveWorkoutDataSource`: same. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkliveworkoutbuilder ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkliveworkoutdatasource
- Setup (Apple "Running workout sessions"): `HKWorkoutConfiguration(activityType: .traditionalStrengthTraining, locationType: .indoor)` → `HKWorkoutSession(healthStore:configuration:)` → `session.associatedWorkoutBuilder()` → `builder.dataSource = HKLiveWorkoutDataSource(healthStore:workoutConfiguration:)` → delegates → `session.startActivity(with:)` + `builder.beginCollection(at:)`. Samples arrive via `workoutBuilder(_:didCollectDataOf:)`; use `builder.statistics(for:)`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/running-workout-sessions
- "All workout sessions generate high-frequency heart rate samples". "while the session runs, Apple Watch automatically saves active energy-burned samples … HealthKit provides optimized calorie calculations for some activities … For all other activities, the system estimates calories based on the data from Apple Watch's sensors. Depending on the activity, this rate is either never lower than the brisk walk burn rate or never lower than the brisk walk burn rate when moving." (same page)
- `functionalStrengthTraining`: "HealthKit provides optimized calorie calculations for this activity". `traditionalStrengthTraining` ("primarily using machines or free weights") has no such statement. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutactivitytype/functionalstrengthtraining ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutactivitytype/traditionalstrengthtraining
- Old forum report (2018, tennis) that the live data source produced 0 calories for some activity/type combos; workaround was an `HKAnchoredObjectQuery` + `builder.add(_:)`. Not reproduced for strength training; keep as a fallback pattern. https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/110498
- **Haptics pause HR sampling:** "Do not call this method while gathering heart rate data using HealthKit. When you engage the haptic engine, HealthKit stops gathering heart rate data until after the haptic engine finishes." https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkinterfacedevice/play(_:)  (Design: short, infrequent rest-timer haptics; expect HR gaps around them.)
- Ending: `session.stopActivity(with:)` → on `.stopped`: `builder.endCollection(at:)` → `builder.finishWorkout()` → `session.end()`. Only `finishWorkout` writes the `HKWorkout`. Metadata: `builder.addMetadata(_:)`; custom samples/events: `builder.add(_:)`, `builder.addWorkoutEvents(_:)`; per-exercise segmentation: `HKWorkoutActivity` via `beginNewActivity(configuration:date:metadata:)` / `endCurrentActivity(on:)` on the session. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutsession ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutbuilder
- Background modes for the watch target: "Workout sessions require the Workout processing background mode. If your app plays audio or provides haptic feedback during the workout session, you must also add the Audio background mode." (Running workout sessions page.)
- Coordinate with companion (Apple guidance on the same page): if the user starts on the watch and tries to end on the phone, the iOS app "should instruct the user to end the workout in your watchOS app"; "If the user doesn't start and stop a workout session in your watchOS app, don't try to retroactively create a workout session on Apple Watch."
- Workout apps that record via HealthKit are auto-suggested in the watchOS 26 Smart Stack: "specify the correct HKWorkoutActivityType, record the accurate start and end time of workouts". https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/334/

### 3.2 Can iOS save workouts it did not run?
- Yes. `HKWorkoutBuilder` has been on iOS since 12 ("Incrementally collect samples and events … call `finishWorkout` … For watchOS, use an `HKWorkoutSession` and an `HKLiveWorkoutBuilder` instead."). https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkworkoutbuilder  Since iOS 26 the phone can also run a live session/builder itself. WWDC25: "always use the workout builder API to create and save a workout. This will ensure that activity rings are appropriately updated." https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/322/
- For Ledger: the watch saves the workout when the session ends; the phone only needs `HKWorkoutBuilder` for a phone-only fallback (no watch paired) or for backfilling manually logged sessions.

### 3.3 Authorization, Info.plist, entitlements, privacy manifest
- Add the HealthKit capability (entitlement `com.apple.developer.healthkit`; `com.apple.developer.healthkit.access` only for clinical records — don't). https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/setting-up-healthkit
- **Both `NSHealthShareUsageDescription` (read) and `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` (write) are required**; "You must set the usage keys, or your app will crash when you request authorization." Since watchOS 6 the watch app shows its own sheet, so **the watch target needs the keys too**. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkhealthstore/requestauthorization(toshare:read:completion:) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/running-workout-sessions
- Enabling HealthKit adds `healthkit` to `UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities` on iOS — remove it if the app should install on devices without HealthKit; "The `healthkit` entry isn't used by watchOS apps." (setting-up-healthkit)
- Request only what you use; the app can't tell denied from granted for reads; iOS 27 adds limited-history grants — call `earliestAuthorizedSampleDate(for:)`. SwiftUI: `.healthDataAccessRequest(store:shareTypes:readTypes:trigger:completion:)` from `HealthKitUI`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data
- **Privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`)**: required for every app; declares `NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes` and `NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypes` (required-reason APIs) on iOS and watchOS. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy-manifest-files  HealthKit is **not** a required-reason API category (categories are file timestamp, system boot time, disk space, active keyboard, user defaults — https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/app-privacy-configuration/nsprivacyaccessedapitypes/nsprivacyaccessedapitype; **UNCONFIRMED** only in the sense that I did not re-read the full category list today). Declare Health/Fitness collected-data types only if health data leaves the device; on-device-only HealthKit use can be labelled "Data Not Collected" (community guidance: https://github.com/wjdavis5/lunarlog/issues/254; example App Store listing https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id949782314). If Ledger syncs sets/reps via CloudKit, that is "Fitness" data collected and linked to the user — declare it in the manifest and the App Store Connect questionnaire.

### 3.4 App Review expectations (Guidelines as of 2026-09-16)
https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- **5.1.3(i):** no use/disclosure of HealthKit data "for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining purposes"; "You must disclose the specific health data that you are collecting from the device."
- **5.1.3(ii):** "Apps must not write false or inaccurate data into HealthKit … and **may not store personal health information in iCloud**." → D8.
- **5.1.2(vi):** HealthKit data may not be used for marketing/advertising/data mining incl. by third parties.
- **2.5.1** (common rejection): "The app uses the HealthKit or CareKit APIs but does not clearly identify the HealthKit and CareKit functionality in the app's user interface" and "does not appear to include any primary features that require health or fitness data". Make the HealthKit integration visible (workout saved to Health, HR shown, toggle in settings). Forum examples: https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/802626 ; https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/762128
- A privacy policy URL is required in App Store Connect and in-app (5.1.1). Live Activities must not be used for spam/marketing (4.5.3).

## 4. Live Activities, Dynamic Island, watch Smart Stack, complications, gestures

### 4.1 ActivityKit basics for a rest timer
- `Activity.request(attributes:content:pushType:)` (iOS 16.2+): "your app needs to be in the foreground to start a Live Activity … However, you can start a Live Activity while your app is in the background by using an app intent that conforms to `LiveActivityIntent`." Update/end are allowed from the background. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/activity/request(attributes:content:pushtype:) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities
- **Mirroring exception:** WWDC23: HealthKit launches the phone app in the background and "gives my app 10 seconds to start a live activity and call a handler to start mirroring." Apple's iOS 26 iPhone-workout sample also presents workout status with Live Activities. https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10023/ ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/building-a-workout-app-for-iphone-and-ipad  → D5: start the single "workout" Live Activity from `workoutSessionMirroringStartHandler`; the rest timer is a *state* of that activity.
- Countdown rendering: `Text(timerInterval:pauseTime:countsDown:showsHours:)` (iOS 16 / watchOS 9) — the system ticks it, no per-second updates needed; in widgets the text becomes horizontally flexible, so constrain with `.frame(width:)` + `.multilineTextAlignment`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/text/init(timerinterval:pausetime:countsdown:showshours:)
- Limits: active up to **8 hours**, then removed from the Dynamic Island; stays on the Lock Screen up to 4 more hours (12 total). `end(_:dismissalPolicy:)` with `.immediate` / `.after(_:)` (max 4 h). Set `staleDate` so a dead phone-side mirror shows as stale. (displaying-live-data doc above)
- Update budget: local `activity.update()` on iOS is not push-budgeted, but **each update also syncs to Apple Watch and counts against the watch budget**; over-budget updates "may not immediately appear when someone's wrist is down". Push updates: priority 10 draws down an hourly budget, priority 5 doesn't; `NSSupportsLiveActivitiesFrequentUpdates` for high-rate cases. Apple engineer on a 3-second-update request: "you can't have both." https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10068/ ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/starting-and-updating-live-activities-with-activitykit-push-notifications ; https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/797554  → update the activity on set-complete / rest-start / rest-end only; let `Text(timerInterval:)` do the ticking.
- **iOS 27 changes (WWDC26 session 223):** Dynamic Island compact/minimal views now show in landscape; new `@Environment(\.isDynamicIslandLimitedInWidth)`; StandBy shows the Lock-Screen view at 200% (use `showsWidgetContainerBackground`, `activityBackgroundTint`); interactive `Button(intent:)` with `LiveActivityIntent` (e.g. "+30 s", "Skip rest", "Done"); `.supplementalActivityFamilies([.small])` for watch/CarPlay. https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/

### 4.2 Live Activities on Apple Watch
- Since iOS 18 / watchOS 11 an iPhone Live Activity "will appear in the Smart Stack on Apple Watch automatically", by default showing the Dynamic Island compact leading/trailing views; customise with `.supplementalActivityFamilies([.small])` + `@Environment(\.activityFamily)`; tap shows a full-screen view with "open on iPhone", or opens your watch app if the watch Info.plist has "Supports Launch for Live Activity Attribute Types"; use `isLuminanceReduced` for Always On; with poor connectivity "Start, End, and alerting updates are prioritized". https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10068/
- Excluding `.small` only removes customisation — the activity still appears in the Smart Stack. Apple DTS + follow-up: https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/762919
- **watchOS 26 auto-launch:** Live Activities now "automatically take over your watch display" on wrist raise; users can turn off Settings > Smart Stack > Live Activities > Auto-Launch Live Activities, per app. https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/apple-watch-live-activities-fix/  Ledger's watch app is frontmost during a workout (HKWorkoutSession), so the phone LA mostly matters *after* the user leaves the watch app; make the `.small` view a one-glance "Bench 3/5 · rest 0:42" so the takeover is helpful, not annoying.
- **A watchOS app cannot start its own Live Activity.** Apple Frameworks Engineer (Jun 2024): "While it's technically possible to use WatchConnectivity to poke your iOS app to start a live activity that then gets forwarded back to the watch, it probably doesn't make sense to do so." No change found in watchOS 26/27. https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/757685  (Double tap can target a button inside a remote Live Activity in the Smart Stack — see 4.4.)

### 4.3 WidgetKit complications / Smart Stack on watchOS
- WidgetKit accessory widgets double as watch complications and Smart Stack widgets (ClockKit is legacy; once you ship a WidgetKit complication the system stops calling ClockKit). https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/creating-accessory-widgets-and-watch-complications
- **watchOS 26:** `RelevanceKit` (watchOS-only effect; imported implicitly with AppIntents): `RelevantContext.fitness(_:)`/`FitnessCondition`, `.date(...)`, `.location(...)`, `.sleep(...)`, `.hardware(headphones:)`; new `RelevanceConfiguration` "Relevant widget" that appears only in the Smart Stack; **Controls** (WidgetKit `ControlWidget`) in Control Center, Smart Stack and the Ultra Action button — "Build a control when the primary purpose is to perform an action" (e.g. "Start next workout"); configurable widgets on watch (`recommendations()` returns `[]` on watchOS 26+). https://developer.apple.com/documentation/relevancekit ; https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/334/
- watchOS 27: `transferCurrentComplicationUserInfo` fixed for WidgetKit complications; Siri Modular face mirrors the top Smart Stack widget. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchos-release-notes/watchos-27-release-notes ; https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/apple-releases-watchos-27/

### 4.4 Gestures and watch-specific workout features
- **Double tap (watchOS 11+, Series 9/Ultra 2 and later):** `.handGestureShortcut(.primaryAction)` on a `Button`/`Toggle`/`NavigationLink`; one primary action per scene; only fires if the control is on screen, otherwise the system scrolls toward it; default behaviours (scroll lists, page vertical TabView) are automatic; works on widgets and Live Activities in the Smart Stack "including remote live activities from iOS". https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchos-apps/enabling-double-tap ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/handgestureshortcut(_:isenabled:)  → Ledger: primary action = "Log set" during a set, "Skip rest" during rest.
- **Wrist flick (watchOS 26):** "There is no separate API for the Wrist Flick gesture." (Apple, WWDC25 watchOS lab) https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/791574
- **watchOS 27 single-handed tap** (open a Smart Stack widget): system feature; no developer API found — **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Workout Buddy:** system feature of Apple's Workout app; no third-party API found in the WWDC26 watchOS guide or release notes — **UNCONFIRMED that any exists**. https://developer.apple.com/wwdc26/guides/watchos/
- Third-party HR monitor note in watchOS 27 release notes: when a third-party HR monitor is active, the watch pauses background HR measurements — irrelevant unless Ledger adds chest-strap support.

## 5. SwiftUI on watchOS 26/27

### 5.1 Navigation
- `NavigationStack` is the standard stack on watch; `TabView` with `.tabViewStyle(.verticalPage)` (watchOS 10+) is the page-based container that binds to the Digital Crown. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/tabviewstyle/verticalpage ; design guidance: https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10138/
- watchOS 27 SDK fixes `TabView.selection` updating only after crossing the 50% page threshold (so selection now changes as soon as the offset moves — audit any code that keyed off selection changes). https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchos-release-notes/watchos-27-release-notes
- Community pitfall: "Nested TabView on watchOS Causes Memory Leaks!" — avoid nesting page TabViews. https://fatbobman.com/en/posts/watchos-development-pitfalls-and-practical-tips
- watchOS 26 Liquid Glass: "Toolbar and control styles have been updated for watchOS 26. All apps built for watchOS 10 and later automatically use the new styles"; audit custom styles for legibility; update icons with Icon Composer. https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/334/  watchOS 27: reordering (`.reorderable()`) arrives on watch; swipe actions in custom scroll layouts. https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/269/

### 5.2 Digital Crown for stepping a weight
- Preferred (watchOS 9+): `digitalCrownRotation(detent:from:through:by:sensitivity:isContinuous:isHapticFeedbackEnabled:onChange:onIdle:)` — "The binding will always be updated to a value that is a multiple of the stride"; `onChange` gives a `DigitalCrownEvent` with `offset` and `velocity`; `onIdle` fires when the crown settles (commit the weight there). The view must be `.focusable()`. Haptic detents are on by default (`isHapticFeedbackEnabled`). Use `sensitivity: .low`/`.medium` for 2.5 kg / 5 lb steps. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/digitalcrownrotation(detent:from:through:by:sensitivity:iscontinuous:ishapticfeedbackenabled:onchange:onidle:) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/digitalcrownevent
- Simpler (watchOS 6+): `digitalCrownRotation(_:from:through:by:sensitivity:isContinuous:isHapticFeedbackEnabled:)`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/digitalcrownrotation(_:from:through:by:sensitivity:iscontinuous:ishapticfeedbackenabled:)
- Tutorial: https://www.hackingwithswift.com/quick-start/swiftui/how-to-read-the-digital-crown-on-watchos-using-digitalcrownrotation

### 5.3 Haptics
- `WKInterfaceDevice.current().play(_:)` with `WKHapticType` (`.start`, `.stop`, `.success`, `.failure`, `.retry`, `.click`, `.directionUp/Down`, `.notification`, …). "This method has no effect when called while … applicationState is either background or inactive. By default, you cannot play haptic feedback in the background. The only exception are apps with an active workout session." Minimum 100 ms between plays; the engine stops HR collection while running. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkinterfacedevice/play(_:) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkhaptictype
- Need the `audio` background mode alongside `workout-processing` for background haptics (Running workout sessions page, §3.1).

### 5.4 Keeping the app frontmost during a workout
- **`HKWorkoutSession` is the right tool; not `WKExtendedRuntimeSession`.** Apple engineer: "Use HKWorkout for workout sessions and WKExtendedRuntime for self-care, mindfulness, physical therapy, or smart alarm sessions." https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/721077  Extended runtime docs: each app supports one session type (self care / mindfulness / physical therapy / smart alarm); "If the physical therapy activity is strenuous … consider using an HKWorkoutSession instead"; sessions have time limits and can be cancelled for CPU use. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/using-extended-runtime-sessions ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkextendedruntimesession
- With an active workout session: "Your app continues to run throughout the entire workout session, even when the user lowers their wrist or interacts with a different app. When the user raises their wrist, your app reappears … If the user navigates back to the watch face, Apple Watch displays a small icon at the top of the screen … Your app can alert the user using audio or haptic feedback while running in the background." Excessive background CPU → suspension. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/running-workout-sessions
- `WKExtension`/`WKExtensionDelegate` deprecated in watchOS 27 SDK (min target ≥ 9.2): use `WKApplication`/`WKApplicationDelegate` for `handle(_ workoutConfiguration:)` and `handleActiveWorkoutRecovery()`. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchos-release-notes/watchos-27-release-notes ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkapplicationdelegate/handleactiveworkoutrecovery()

### 5.5 Always On
- watchOS 8+: the UI stays visible on wrist-down; frontmost apps stay up to the user's "Return to Clock" setting (max 1 h), but "Apps running a background session, such as a workout session … remain onscreen as long as the session is active" at a reduced refresh rate. Pause animations, drop sub-second updates; drive periodic UI with `TimelineView` and check `context.cadence` (`.live`/`.seconds`/`.minutes`); observe `scenePhase`. Use `@Environment(\.isLuminanceReduced)` to dim/outline; `.privacySensitive()` / `redactionReasons.contains(.privacy)` to hide health data ("always hide any highly sensitive information, such as financial information or health data"). `WKSupportsAlwaysOnDisplay` = false opts out. Xcode simulator has a Toggle Always On button. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchos-apps/designing-your-app-for-the-always-on-state ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/isluminancereduced
- Rest timer in Always On: `Text(timerInterval:)` / `Text(_:style: .timer)` keep updating for free in Always On ("the system automatically updates the Text view while in Always On").
- Community practice for long workouts: 1 Hz while the wrist is up, 10–20 s cadence when `isLuminanceReduced`. https://fatbobman.com/en/posts/watchos-development-pitfalls-and-practical-tips
